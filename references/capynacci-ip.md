# Capynacci IP

## Character Definition

Capynacci is the fixed visual IP for body text illustrations in articles, technical writeups, and Notion documents by Algonacci.

Capynacci is not a decorative mascot, sticker, or cute cartoon, but an Applied AI Researcher and Tech Founder seriously, calmly, and bizarrely operating complex systems and explaining concepts.

## Appearance

- **Body**: Classic chill capybara body shape with warm golden-brown fur and a distinct brown snout.
- **Eyewear**: Smart round spectacles (black wire frames).
- **Outfit**: Signature dark navy tech hoodie.
- **Footwear**: Subtle athletic running shoes (nod to Ironman/endurance sports).
- **Expression**: Calm, confident, unbothered, *deadpan-wise*.
- **Rendering**: Richly colored warm tones that pop iconically against clean white backgrounds.

## Personality & Background

- **Role**: Founder & Research Director at Braincore Research Labs / Applied AI Systems Engineer.
- **Mindset**: Systems thinker, bridge between R&D experimentation and production SaaS execution.
- **Vibe**: Extremely calm under pressure (unbothered by CUDA OOMs, model hallucinations, or server crashes).
- **Humor**: Deadpan, sharp, absurd, yet technically accurate.

## Common Duties & Action Pool

Make Capynacci the subject of the core action:
- Turning physical weight dials or tuning hyperparameter knobs on neural network graphs.
- Routing data pipelines from GPU clusters directly into product engines (Omniflow).
- Inspecting model loss signals or debugging containerized pipelines.
- Standing on a small step stool with a wooden pointer stick explaining system architectures.
- Carrying or joining complex R&D modules into enterprise product frameworks.

## Restrictions

- Do NOT draw Capynacci as a plain black silhouette (Capynacci must have warm brown fur and signature outfit).
- Do NOT draw Capynacci as a childish cartoon character or meme.
- Do NOT let Capynacci just stand in the corner as passive decoration; Capynacci must perform the core action.
- Do NOT make the background complex, dark, or textured; the canvas must be pure white.
