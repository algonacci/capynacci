# Style DNA

## Summary

Pure white background, minimalist black doodle line-art diagrams, generous white space, restrained handwritten annotations, quirky tech product sketch feel.

It looks like an explanatory whiteboard sketch drawn by an Applied AI Researcher and Tech Founder who builds production AI systems and scalable software.

## Must Have

- **Format**: 16:9 horizontal body text illustration.
- **Canvas**: Pure white background (`#FFFFFF`): no paper texture, no gradients, no shadows, no retro yellowing.
- **Diagram Line Art**: Minimalist black hand-drawn line art (thin lines, slightly wobbly pen feel, non-mechanical).
- **Featured Character**: Capynacci fully rendered in warm colors (golden-brown fur, dark navy tech hoodie, glasses, running shoes) to contrast iconically against the black-and-white background diagram.
- **White Space**: Generous white space (main subject occupies ~40%-60% of canvas, with at least 35% clean blank space).
- **Annotations**: Target 3-5 handwritten English labels (8 maximum). Short, clear phrases (2-6 words).

## Color System

- **Character (Capynacci)**: Golden-brown fur, dark navy hoodie, black glasses.
- **Diagram Line Art & Main Text**: Black.
- **Orange**: Main data flow, model prediction paths, automation direction, R&D-to-Product pipelines.
- **Red**: Key warnings, loss signals, errors, bottlenecks, critical reminders.
- **Blue**: Weights adjustment, system state, cloud/infrastructure notes, memory/GPU metrics.

## Absolutely Do Not

- NO commercial vector clipart.
- NO corporate PPT slide look.
- NO formal engineering CAD diagrams.
- NO dark mode backgrounds or heavy gradients.
- NO realistic high-tech UI screenshots.
- NO titles in the top-left corner (e.g. "Workflow / Architecture / Roadmap").
- NO overcrowded diagrams explaining every single micro-node.
